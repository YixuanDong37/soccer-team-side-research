  held-out trade-off table by margin, the per-clip tables, v_robust's guard tables (r9) and

- So the DESIGN and the RULE below are informed by round-0 held-out results of a similar method.
  What is blind is only: the results of the changed method (tiers, solid-only position table,
  leaves) under the rule below. The 37 reserved clips remain the clean test.

A. METHOD CHANGES (fixed now, no parameter searched)
A1. Tiers. After m_cuts' cuts (P >= 0.7, unchanged) a (sub-)piece is SOLID when it has at least
    5 boxes, otherwise SHORT. 5 is the lab's existing constant (score.py MIN_MATCHED, the
    "fragment" class of the diagnosis); it is not tuned. Sensitivity rows for 3 and 10 boxes
    will be reported but not selected.
A2. Stage 1 (core): fuse's agglomeration (linker.py, unchanged rule: appearance floor 0.3,
    "nobody" rival 0.5, mutual best, margin against co-visible rivals) on SOLID pieces only.
    Position evidence for stage 1 is m_space's model run on the solid pieces only (short pieces
    are taken out of the tracks table handed to m_space, so a 1-4 box fragment cannot absorb the
    position evidence between two long pieces). Plugin bonus as in fuse, on the solid pieces.
    Pair model: logistic regression on (cos_w, sink_lo, plug) fitted per fold on the solid-pair
    table with weak labels (>= 1 matched row), as in fuse.
A3. Stage 2 (leaves): a SHORT piece may be attached to at most one stage-1 group (or single
    solid piece), never to another short piece, and it never connects two groups. Evidence:
    appearance cosine of the short piece against the group's pooled prototype (the group's
    prototype is not changed by leaves) + position bonus at its junctions with the group's SOLID
    members (m_space on all pieces, same pair-model weight ratio as the core); NO plugin bonus for
    leaves (m_keep / fuse diagnosis: plugin joins of 1-4 box pieces are more often wrong than right).
    Same rule and the same margin as the core (floor 0.3, "nobody" 0.5, mutual best, rivals).
    Leaves on / off is a candidate setting chosen by the rule in B, per operating point, per fold.
A4. Everything else as in fuse (m_cuts P >= 0.7, m_space weights of the fold, t_abs 0.5).

B. OPERATING POINTS: RULE (fixed now)
Candidates: leaves in {off, on} x margin in (0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50,
0.55, 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, 0.90, 1.00, 1.20) (fuse's grid).
Per fold, on the 12 fit clips, with the fold's own weights, for a point with cap K and penalty lam
(strict K = 4, lam = 10; balanced K = 7, lam = 3; recall K = 14, lam = 1; the lam values are
round 0's, the caps are the task's):
 (i)  hard constraint: wrong returns on the fit clips <= floor(K * scored_fit / 137) at the
      candidate's margin AND at every stricter margin with the same leaves setting;
 (ii) among the candidates that meet (i), the largest
        J = sum over the fit clips except d* of [ right - lam * w ],
      w (per clip) = wrong returns + links judged wrong by score.py + leaf links whose two pieces
      have different majority annotated persons (weak labels, >= 1 matched row on both sides),
      d* = the fit clip with the largest w for that candidate (first in DEV_CLIPS order on ties);
 (iii) ties: the larger margin, then leaves off.
 If no candidate meets (i): fewest wrong returns on the fit clips, then (ii), then (iii).
The chosen candidate is applied unchanged to the held-out clip; the 13 held-out clips are pooled.
The frozen method = the same rule on all 13 clips with the all-13 weights.
Why this rule: the cap is the task's definition of a point; the exchange rate lam keeps a fold whose
fit clips happen to be clean from taking the loosest margin; leaving out the worst fit clip in J
keeps one clip from dictating the threshold of all others (the cap in (i) still counts every clip).

C. WHAT WILL BE REPORTED WHATEVER COMES OUT
- Headline: the pooled held-out counts of the three points under rule B (score.py CLI on the METHOD
  files), per clip, margins / leaves chosen per fold, and whether the limits 4 / 7 / 14 hold.
- Next to each point: the held-out row of the FROZEN candidate (the all-13 choice applied to every
  clip with that clip's fold weights), so that "what the rule did" and "what the frozen threshold
  does" can be told apart.
- The same pooled result under alternative rules, all computed from the same runs:
  R0 = round-0 rule (J, lam 10 / 3 / 1, no trimming, no cap); Rcap = cap only (most right under (i));
  Rnotrim = rule B without leaving a clip out. And rule B on round 0's linker (no tiers).
- The full fixed-candidate trade-off table (held-out and in-sample), not used to pick anything.
- Linking is compared with CUTS ONLY (0 right / 0 wrong / 137 lost, 1.00% impure rows), not only
  with gbf now: impure rows added by links, per clip.
- Clip bootstrap, results without SNGS-162, each threshold moved by +-10%.
- A stress test with degraded embeddings and a label-free self-check, as the answer to the risk
  that PRTReid was trained on these clips (they cannot remove that risk).
